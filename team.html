  • Janet Antich Animator

    Janet Antich (the-ink-spot) is a multimedia artist and animator. She holds a B.S. in Architectural Studies from the University of Texas at Austin, and an M.F.A. in Painting and Drawing from Pratt Institute. She specializes in animation and motion graphics for clients ranging from advertising agencies and marketing firms to experimental theater groups, musicians, and independent filmmakers. Drawn to experiments with perception - illusion, distortion, the potential of a line to be a plane / dot / curve / fold - her work combines frame by frame drawings, photography, and tactile objects, utilizing both analog (pre-film) and digital techniques.
